Question: can landmarks from tutorial videos feed or test the recognizer without recruiting signers?
Three videos (Learn How to Sign `0FcwzMq4iWg`, standard YouTube licence; LearnSigns Family
`3hyOh9UD4jw` and Colors `csqLvE2NeBU`, CC BY as labelled). Stage 1, label-free segmentation
(`probe_stage1.py`, the decoder's endpointer ported): 98 of 99 caption anchors matched; release model
top-1 21, top-5 35 over 250 classes; masked to each video's signs top-1 41 (42%) vs 6 to 12% chance;
new-signer videos 29% and 38%. Reading: usable domain with a real gap, not an accuracy number; the
first probe (14/16) was optimistic. Tools: `extract_landmarks.py` (MediaPipe Tasks, mirrors `frame.ts`),
`probe_25.py`, `probe_stage1.py`, `finetune_loo.py` + `ml/configs/research-youtube-finetune.yaml`.

Leave-one-video-out fine-tune (Kaggle-only baseline `seeds/runs/seed1` + clips from the other videos +
Kaggle replay, replay-only control, 3 seeds, 19 min on CPU): **no measurable gain**. Masked top-1 on the
held-out video, base / control / fine-tuned: 20 / 19.0 / 19.0, 9 / 9.0 / 8.3, 12 / 11.7 / 13.3 anchors;
Kaggle sample val top-1 fell 0.684 to 0.645 to 0.667. About 65 noisy clips from at most two signers are
far too few to test the idea. Output `research-videos/finetune_loo.json`; details in the report.
