# Lexicon, data and community-review rules

- `lexicon/core/` is SignLnk's own CC BY 4.0 content only. ASL-LEX (CC BY-NC 4.0) and ASL Signbank
  (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) data stay in `lexicon/reference/` or as external links; never in bundles.
- Lexicon review: an edit needs 1 reviewer; a new sign needs 2, at least one Deaf reviewer.
- `docs/datasets.md` entries follow the Kaggle ISLR entry's fields and quote the licence from the
  primary source with URL and date (`/dataset-license`).
- Consent forms separate landmarks, video, public clip and research-only, and allow withdrawal.
- `docs/review/` holds sign-offs for gates G0–G5. Never write or alter a sign-off on someone's
  behalf; drafts of checklists are fine.
- These files are licence- and people-sensitive: edits ask for approval.
